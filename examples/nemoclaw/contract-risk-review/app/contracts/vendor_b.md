# Data Processing Agreement — Vendor B (Bluefin Analytics Inc.)

> Synthetic contract for the PraisonAI × OpenShell example. Not legal advice.

**B-1.1 Scope.** Vendor B processes Customer usage events to produce weekly analytics dashboards.

**B-2.1 Term.** 12 months, renewing for 12 months unless either party gives 30 days' notice.

**B-3.1 Liability.** Each party's liability is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

**B-4.1 Data location.** Vendor B may store and process Customer Data in any country where Vendor B or its affiliates operate, including outside the EU.

**B-4.2 Sub-processors.** Vendor B may appoint or replace sub-processors at any time without notice to the Customer.

**B-4.3 Security incidents.** Vendor B will notify the Customer of a personal data breach within a commercially reasonable time.

**B-5.1 Ownership.** The Customer retains all rights in Customer Data.

**B-6.1 Audit.** The Customer may audit Vendor B once per year on 30 days' notice.
