# Master Services Agreement — Vendor A (Acme Hosting Ltd.)

> Synthetic contract for the PraisonAI × OpenShell example. Not legal advice.

**A-1.1 Services.** Vendor A provides managed hosting for the Customer's internal ticketing system.

**A-2.1 Fees.** Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears and payable within 30 days.

**A-3.1 Term.** The initial term is 12 months from the Effective Date.

**A-3.2 Renewal.** This Agreement renews automatically for successive 36-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

**A-4.1 Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's Confidential Information confidential for 5 years after termination.

**A-5.1 Liability.** Vendor A's aggregate liability is limited to the fees paid in the preceding 1 month. The Customer's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

**A-6.1 Data location.** Customer Data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) region.

**A-7.1 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland.
