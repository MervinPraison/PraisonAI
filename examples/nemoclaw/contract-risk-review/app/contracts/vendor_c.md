# Software Licence Agreement — Vendor C (Cobalt AI GmbH)

> Synthetic contract for the PraisonAI × OpenShell example. Not legal advice.

**C-1.1 Licence.** Vendor C grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use the Cobalt forecasting platform.

**C-2.1 Term.** 12 months, renewing for 12 months unless either party gives 30 days' notice.

**C-3.1 Liability.** Each party's liability is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

**C-4.1 Data location.** Customer Data is stored in the EU (Dublin) region.

**C-5.1 Customer Data.** The Customer owns the Customer Data it uploads.

**C-5.2 Derived data.** Vendor C owns all models, aggregates and other derivatives created from Customer Data and may use them for any purpose.

**C-6.1 Fees.** Vendor C may increase the fees unilaterally by up to 15% per contract year.

**C-7.1 Termination for convenience.** Vendor C may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 30 days' notice. The Customer may terminate only for material breach.

**C-8.1 Support.** Support is available 09:00–17:00 CET on business days.
