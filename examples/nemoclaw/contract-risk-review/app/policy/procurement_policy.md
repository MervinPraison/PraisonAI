# Procurement Policy — Vendor Contracts

> Synthetic policy for the PraisonAI × OpenShell example.

Every vendor clause that breaks a rule below is a violation. Use the stated severity.

| Rule | Requirement | Severity if violated |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** Renewal | Automatic renewal terms must be 12 months or less, and the non-renewal notice period must be 30 days or less. | Medium |
| **P2** Liability | Liability caps must be mutual, and neither party may carry unlimited liability. A vendor cap below 12 months of fees is a violation. | High |
| **P3** Data protection | Customer Data must stay in the EU, and the vendor must notify personal data breaches within 72 hours. | High |
| **P4** Sub-processors | The vendor must give at least 30 days' prior written notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor. | Medium |
| **P5** Data ownership | The Customer must own its data and all derivatives of it. | High |
| **P6** Commercial terms | Price increases are capped at 5% per year, and termination for convenience must be available to both parties equally. | Medium |
